Making of

Momento is an album of love songs, written for oneself and for others. It came out of a year of mostly non-musical experiences: twenty-one-year-old emotions, nights lit up by the capital, fights with self-awareness. Momento is “love thy neighbour as thyself,” caught between narcissism and tenderness.

Written and produced by Policrom · Photography by Attilio Cusani · Mixed and mastered by Fiorenzo Serino
