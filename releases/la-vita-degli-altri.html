Making of

La Vita degli Altri is the sea seen from afar, then from below — a memory forgotten by heart, a house perched over the canyon, the desire for other people’s lives.

Written by Giuseppe Donatiello and Gianluca Errico (Policrom) · Produced and mixed by Cosmo (Marco Jacopo Bianchi) · Mastered by Andrea Suriani (Alpha Dept. Studio) · Label: Morgana · Artwork: VictorSoldier
