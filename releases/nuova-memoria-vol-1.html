Nuova Memoria Vol. 1

Making of

Nuova Memoria was born out of the intent to capture and leave a trace of a sonic present that feels too fast to grasp, to really think through. We gathered a group of musicians and friends who make electronic music in Rome, who love playing with machines, computers and turntables. We wanted to build an open digital archive, create connections and hybridizations, experiment together, blur the lines between us.

The final product is an 8-track compilation condensing all of that — a process that took more than a year. Still evolving.
