Bio

Electronic music producer and psychoanalyst based in Rome.

Since 2010 he has released three EPs and an album as Policrom, including a production collaboration with Cosmo on the production of La Vita degli Altri (2016). Since 2021 he has been producing under the alias Andrea Alice.

His work is built on synthesizers, craftsmanship, and a DIY approach. He explores sound as a collective aggregator — a meeting place for desires, a crossing point for possible futures.
